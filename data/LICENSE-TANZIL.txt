The original Arabic verse text is preserved verbatim in quran.js.
The XML stores the introductory basmala separately on the first verse of chapters 2–114 (except chapter 9); these are preserved as separate surah.bismillah metadata and are not inserted into the verse text. The basmala is verse 1 of chapter 1.
